Head to head

Changes are against the fixed wing. Added weight goes on top of the gross weight, so payload is unchanged. The ceiling uses whichever in-flight shape climbs better.

Cruise: power needed vs airspeed

Where a curve crosses the power-available line is that shape's top speed. Low and left is induced drag (lift costs more at low speed and short span); high and right is parasite drag (every square foot of wing and strut you drag along).
