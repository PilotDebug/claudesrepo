Lift curve

Thin-airfoil theory: lift rises 2π per radian (about 0.11 per degree) from each section's zero-lift angle. Camber moves the line up; thickness doesn't change it in this theory. It predicts no drag and no stall; real sections stall somewhere around 12–16°. It's a learning tool, not a design tool.

Reynolds number

Sea-level standard air (ν = 1.46 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s). Real section data is published per Reynolds number: compare against polars at a similar value.
